How It Works

Step-by-Step Vehicle Service Journey

No guessing games, no forgotten repair requests. See exactly how our system tracks your automobile from initial request to delivery.

1
Step 1 — Online Booking

Digital Service Request

Vehicle owners can register their vehicle details (make, model, license number) and select a specialized service type along with preferred appointment date and symptom descriptions.

  • Record created in MySQL `services` table with status `Pending`
  • Multi-vehicle fleet support for single customer accounts
  • Instant confirmation visible in customer portal
Tip: Describe strange sounds or warning lights to help our diagnostic technicians prepare bay equipment in advance.
2
Step 2 — Bay Inspection

Computerized Diagnostic & Quote

Upon vehicle arrival, our lead technicians conduct a comprehensive 40-point inspection and connect OBD-II diagnostic scanners to analyze live ECU fault codes.

  • Admin approves service and assigns a dedicated mechanic
  • Transparent service charge calculated and updated in MySQL
  • Customer status transitions to `Approved`
No hidden fees: Customer views the exact service charge before any work begins.
3
Step 3 — Precision Repair

Factory-Grade Workshop Service

The assigned mechanic logs into the dedicated Mechanic Bay dashboard, views the complaint log, transitions status to `In Progress`, and carries out the repair.

  • Only genuine OEM replacement parts fitted
  • Mechanic types real-time progress notes directly into the database
  • Customer can refresh their dashboard to view real-time notes
Quality assurance: All torque settings strictly follow OEM factory manuals.
4
Step 4 — Final Delivery

Road Test & Digital Settlement

The vehicle undergoes an exhaustive road test, complimentary foam wash, and sanitized handover. Mechanic marks the service `Completed`.

  • Digital invoice synced directly to customer portal
  • Instant settlement via UPI / QR, Card, or Cash
  • 6-Month / 10,000 km warranty automatically activated
Digital Records: Invoices and service history remain saved permanently in MySQL for resale proof.
